Coming to PC · November 2027

Unsound is a first-person psychological horror game. Explore unsettling environments, solve puzzles, and uncover a dark mystery. Things are not always as they seem, and the deeper you go, the stranger things become. Pay attention to your surroundings and piece together the clues.

Story

You play as Clark Anderson, a university student living in room 305 who works part-time as a security guard for Liminal Legacy.

On your way to work, you realize that you're trapped on your own floor, with seemingly no way out. As you explore the building, familiar places become unrecognizable, and the world around you becomes increasingly strange.

Discover the building's history, and search for a way out.

Environment

You find yourself trapped in a dormitory, rebuilt after a catastrophe. The building seems to have a mind of its own and wants to keep you inside. The deeper you descend, the more it changes. Hallways grow dirtier and more distorted. Rooms turn into places that shouldn't exist as reality begins to fall apart. Each environment draws you further into the unknown, revealing another piece of the story.

Puzzles

Puzzles are central to the story of Unsound. To uncover the truth, you must pay attention to your surroundings, piece together clues, and make sense of what you discover. No markers, no quest log, no instructions. Everything you need is already there. You just have to notice it.

Entities

Something is out there. Or maybe it's all in your head. As reality begins to fall apart, it's hard to tell what's real and what's not. Keep your eyes open and question what you see. Whatever you do, don't get caught.
