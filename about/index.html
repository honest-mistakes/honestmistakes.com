About us

Honest Mistakes is an indie game studio of three people from Germany. We work on Unsound in our spare time, next to our day jobs. It's our first game, and hopefully not our last.

All three of us do a bit of everything. One leans toward art and story, one toward sound and one toward the technical side.

Why Honest Mistakes? Every developer ships mistakes, whether they want to or not. We found that funny because it's true, and we'd rather stand by ours than pretend there aren't any.
